Privacy

Short disclosure for paid beta — not legal advice. Last updated 2026-09-06.

Tripward is local-first. Deterministic protection for Claude Code does not require an account or cloud sync.

What stays on your machine

Journals, sealed receipts, Git checkpoints, and policy under .fusecap/. Private local receipt.html / recovery.html are for you. They do not phone home.

What we collect

  • Local artifacts you create by running Tripward on your machine.
  • If you pay: Stripe sees the email and payment method you type on their Payment Link. We do not store card numbers in receipts or our lander.
  • If you join the waitlist on tripward.dev: the email you submit.

What we do not collect

  • Prompts, completions, file contents, or repository source.
  • Invented USD. Receipts show Actual | Estimate | Unavailable. Claude Code subscription traffic is Unavailable.
  • Host paths and repository fingerprints in shareable artifacts — use tripward receipt --redact.
  • Stripe secret keys, webhook secrets, or API keys.

Sharing

If you send evidence, send redacted JSON — not .env, transcripts, prompts, or repo contents. See SUPPORT.md and SECURITY.md.

Telemetry default is off. No account is required for local protection.
